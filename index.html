TelemetryX

Privacy Policy — Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. Overview

TelemetryX ("the App") is a mobile application that visualizes in-vehicle telemetry in real time: G-force (G-G diagram), speed, 0-100/200/400 km/h sprint timing, and GPS route tracking. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

The short version: all data is created and stored on your device only. The App has no backend server, no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. Nothing you record is ever uploaded or shared with us or anyone else.

2. Data the App Uses

  • Precise location (GPS). Required to record your route and cross-check speed. Access is requested only while you use the App and is not used in the background.
  • On-device motion sensor data. Accelerometer readings are processed locally to compute G-force and assist speed estimation. Sensor data never leaves your device.

3. Google Play Services (Location)

On devices with Google Play Services, the App obtains location through Google's Fused Location API. Google's location service may use Wi-Fi and cell tower signals to help determine your position; that signal processing is handled by Google on the device platform and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. TelemetryX does not access, collect, or receive that data — we only receive the resulting position on your device.

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

The App contains none of the following:

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising or ad identifiers
  • No crash reporting or telemetry upload
  • No user accounts, login, or personal identification
  • No data synchronization or cloud storage

Your recorded sessions (speed traces, G-force data, GPS routes, sprint times) exist only in the App's local storage on your device.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • Revoke location access: Android Settings → Apps → TelemetryX → Permissions → Location. Without it, route tracking and GPS speed are unavailable; G-force display still works.
  • Delete your data: Settings → "Clear all sessions" removes everything stored in the App.
  • Take your data with you: export any session as GPX or JSON at any time.
  • Uninstall: removing the App deletes all locally stored data.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App collects no personal information at all (see sections 2 and 4), there is nothing for us to hold on a child's behalf.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be indicated by a new "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
